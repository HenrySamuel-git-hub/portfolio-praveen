Academic Work

Evaluation of Environmental Impact on Ground Water Quality — Tenali, Guntur (Apr 2023)

Tools used: QGIS, spatial interpolation, field sampling, data analysis.

Conducted a comprehensive study to evaluate the environmental impact on groundwater quality in Tenali, Andhra Pradesh, India, by collecting and analyzing water samples from 20 bore points across Ward No. 30.

Generated spatial distribution maps using QGIS software by integrating attribute data (water quality parameters like chlorides, pH, TDS, conductivity, alkalinity, and total hardness) and field data to identify areas with concentrated water quality issues.

Findings revealed that the groundwater quality in the studied area was within acceptable limits according to WHO standards, with most stations having excellent water quality index scores, except for two stations with good water quality.
